## PDMX-derived fields and source files

The catalog retains metadata and paths from PDMX. PDMX and every underlying score remain governed by the PDMX dataset record and the per-row `license` / `license_url` fields. This repository does not override those terms.

The build requires PDMX’s `subset:no_license_conflict` flag, but users must still review the applicable source record before redistribution or commercial use.

## Audio renders

A rendered WAV/FLAC/MP3 is derived from the selected MIDI and the chosen SoundFont. Its permitted use depends on both the score/MIDI license and the SoundFont license. This repository does not distribute a SoundFont.

## Human recordings

Commercial or third-party human recordings are not mirrored. `has_custom_audio` is treated only as candidate metadata and does not establish ownership, public-domain status, or edition-level score fidelity.
